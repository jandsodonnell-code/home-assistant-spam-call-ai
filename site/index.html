Spam Call AI

Spam Call AI is a personal call-screening assistant used with Home Assistant, Twilio, OpenAI, and Google Contacts.

What it does

  • Uses a trusted-caller list to let known contacts bypass AI screening.
  • Collects a caller's name, callback number, and reason for calling.
  • Flags and locally blocks callers identified as spam, scam, robocall, or unwanted solicitation.
  • Uses Google Contacts read-only access to identify contacts placed in the configured trusted-caller label.

This is a personal-use application. It does not sell user data or use Google Contacts for advertising.
